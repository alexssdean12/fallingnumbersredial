Privacy Policy

Falling Numbers: Redial · Effective November 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Falling Numbers: Redial does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Scores

High scores and the names you enter are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Ads

When you choose to watch an ad for a Redial, Google AdMob may collect your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device identifiers, crash and performance data, and ad interactions to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking when the app asks, ads may be personalized. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, the purchase is processed by Apple and recorded by RevenueCat so it can be restored on your devices. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, not your name or Apple Account details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
