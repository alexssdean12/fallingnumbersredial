Questions

How do I play?

Numbers drop from the top of the screen. Press the matching key on the keypad before each one reaches the bottom line. A number reaching the bottom or a wrong key is a miss. Three misses ends the game. Each level drops more numbers, faster.

What is a Redial?

After your third miss you can Redial once per game to get one miss back. Press # to watch a short ad, or * for no thanks. If you've bought Remove Ads, the Redial is free.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. How do I get it back?

On the start screen, press * to open the shop, then press 0 to Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I stop personalized ads?

Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when the game asks, or turn it off any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Where are my high scores saved?

Only on your iPhone. They aren't sent anywhere, and they're removed if you delete the app.
